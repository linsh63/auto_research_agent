# Support

Auto Research Agent is a personal open-source research project. Support is provided through GitHub issues and discussions on a best-effort basis, without service-level guarantees.

Use issues for reproducible defects, documentation errors and bounded feature proposals. Include versions, a minimal example, expected and observed behavior, and redacted logs. Use discussions for usage questions and research-design conversation. Use the private route in `SECURITY.md` for vulnerabilities and the route in `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` for conduct reports.

Maintainers cannot provide guarantees about research validity, legal or regulatory compliance, access to third-party APIs, recovery of unbacked-up data, private deployment operations, or custom model and dataset support. Users remain responsible for reviewing research decisions, licenses, ethics, data governance, costs and released claims.
