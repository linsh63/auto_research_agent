# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in this project free from harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, sexual identity or orientation.

## Expected behavior

Use welcoming and inclusive language, respect differing viewpoints, accept constructive feedback, focus on what is best for the community, and show empathy. Research disagreement should address evidence, methods and claims rather than people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, sexualized language or attention, insults, intimidation, sustained disruption, publication of private information, or conduct that would reasonably be inappropriate in a professional setting is unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where practical, and protect affected people.

Report conduct incidents through the private contact route in `SECURITY.md`. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. Maintainers will review reports in good faith, avoid unnecessary disclosure, and disclose conflicts of interest.

## Scope and attribution

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly. It is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1, available at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html.
